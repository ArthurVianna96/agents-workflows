# Grill with docs

> Locally adapted from Matt Pocock’s [grill-with-docs](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/main/skills/engineering/grill-with-docs/SKILL.md), retrieved 2026-08-19. Copyright © 2026 Matt Pocock; MIT licensed. See [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## Purpose

Sharpen an idea, plan, or design through a focused interview while recording durable domain language and decisions.

## When to use

Use before a substantial change when terminology, ownership, architecture, boundaries, or trade-offs are unresolved.

## Required context

- The idea or decision to examine.
- Relevant repository guidance, existing behavior, glossary, and decision records.
- The intended audience and constraints.

## Workflow

1. Inspect the repository before asking for facts it already contains.
2. Ask one consequential question at a time. Offer a recommendation when the evidence supports one.
3. Test the emerging design against user journeys, edge cases, existing constraints, and alternatives.
4. Define or clarify durable domain terms in the project glossary when one exists.
5. Record hard-to-reverse decisions and their trade-offs in the project’s decision-record format when one exists.
6. Stop when the decision tree is resolved enough to plan or specify the change. Do not turn the interview into an endless naming exercise.

## Expected output

Provide the resolved design, explicit decisions and open questions, glossary or decision-record updates made, and a recommended next action. Use the shared handoff contract when transferring the work.

## Completion criteria

The key design branches are resolved or explicitly deferred, durable terminology is consistent, and the next planning or specification step has enough context to proceed.
